Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a provider?

Open the Find a Provider page and use the search bar and filters for specialty, insurance, and distance.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the Book button on a provider card. In this class project, booking actions are for navigation and demonstration.

What insurance is accepted?

Each provider card lists sample accepted insurance plans.

When should I call 911?

Call 911 for life threatening symptoms such as severe chest pain, breathing problems, or major injuries.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page and submit the message form.
