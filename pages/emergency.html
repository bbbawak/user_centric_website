Emergency Services

In life threatening emergencies, call 911 immediately.

Do not use online contact forms or email for emergency help.

When to Call 911

  • Chest pain or trouble breathing
  • Severe bleeding
  • Stroke symptoms or sudden confusion
  • Serious injury or loss of consciousness

Urgent Care Guidance

  • Minor fractures and sprains
  • Fever, flu symptoms, and infections
  • Nonsevere cuts requiring stitches
  • After hours care for nonemergencies
